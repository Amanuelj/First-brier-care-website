Our Services

Personalized care and support in a comfortable, family-style home environment.

24/7 Professionalized Care

At MBK Senior Services, LLC, we provide attentive care throughout the day and night. Our goal is to make sure residents feel safe, respected, comfortable, and supported.

Care is personalized according to each resident's individual needs, preferences, abilities, and daily routine.

Personal Care & Daily Living

Support with everyday personal care and activities while encouraging residents to maintain as much independence as possible.

Medication Support

Assistance with medication routines according to the resident's care needs and applicable care requirements.

Meals & Nutrition

Nutritious meals and snacks are provided while considering individual preferences and dietary needs.

Housekeeping & Laundry

We help maintain a clean, comfortable, and welcoming home environment.

Appointments & Health Support

We help residents stay organized with appointments and coordinate care needs as appropriate.

Activities & Engagement

Residents are encouraged to participate in activities that support social connection, movement, enjoyment, and quality of life.

Personalized Daily Support

Every resident has different needs. Our approach is centered around individualized attention and respectful support.

  • Assistance with activities of daily living
  • Monitoring and encouragement when needed
  • Support with mobility and safe movement
  • Personalized routines and daily schedules
  • Social interaction and companionship
  • Support for maintaining independence
  • Communication with families and appropriate care professionals

The Best Care in a Comfortable Environment

We believe that quality care should feel personal. Our home environment is designed to provide comfort, safety, dignity, and a sense of belonging.

About Our Home

MBK Senior Services, LLC provides care in a small home setting where residents can receive individualized attention and develop genuine relationships with caregivers.

What Makes Us Special

Our small home environment allows us to focus on each resident rather than treating everyone the same. We value kindness, respect, patience, and personalized attention.

Our Philosophy

We believe every person deserves to be treated with compassion, respect, and dignity. We strive to do what is right and good for each resident while recognizing their individual value and preferences.

Daily Life

We encourage residents to enjoy a balanced daily routine that includes care, rest, social connection, activities, and personal choice.

Depending on individual preferences and schedules, a typical day may include breakfast, quiet time, lunch, appointments or family visits, chair exercises, reading, movies, arts and crafts, and activities selected by residents.

Our goal is to create a home where residents can feel comfortable and participate in activities that bring meaning and enjoyment to their day.

About Owners & Staff

Our care team is committed to providing respectful, dependable, and individualized support to every resident.

Management has a background that includes a Bachelor of Science in Exercise and Nutritional Science with a minor in Psychology, along with experience in skilled nursing and home health.

This background supports our focus on helping residents maintain independence, mobility, comfort, and quality of life.
